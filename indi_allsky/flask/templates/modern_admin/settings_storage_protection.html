Disk availability

For an external drive, use a persistent UUID mount, set the image directory, and rerun the installer to align capture and web paths. Then enable this check. Capture waits for the configured disk at startup; media access and cleanup report its absence instead of using the SD card.

Changing this setting does not format, mount or move any data. Disabling the check removes this protection.

Automatic cleanup

Cleanup order: daytime images first, then night images, then daytime generated outputs, and finally night timelapses and other night outputs. The oldest eligible files in each group are removed first, stopping at your free-space target. Images include FITS, RAW, panoramas and their thumbnails.

Generated outputs keep the configured timelapse retention period, with a minimum of five days. Recent images, calibration files and configuration are protected. Pending uploads and generations can postpone cleanup. If eligible files cannot free enough space, a notification warns you; retention limits are never shortened automatically.
